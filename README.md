## 🧭 About Me

Senior **Blockchain Architect**, **Distributed Systems Engineer**, and **Lead SDET** specializing in regulated DeFi, atomic cross-chain settlement (HTLC & DvP), self-custody cryptography, and autonomous AI agents.

Obsessed with **zero-trust architecture**, mathematical correctness, automated formal verification, and engineering high-throughput infrastructure that operates flawlessly under financial-grade load.

## ⚡ What I Do

- **Cross-Chain DeFi & Bridges** — atomic swaps (DvP), HTLC escrow bridges (Canton ↔ EVM, TRON, Solana), and automated market makers (AMM / dynamic spread).
- **Quality Engineering & SDET** — bulletproof test automation suites (Vitest, Jest, Playwright, Synpress), incremental mutation coverage gates, and AST-level static architecture analyzers.
- **Self-Custody & Cryptography** — WebAuthn/Passkey zero-knowledge challenges, BIP-39 mnemonic key derivation, Ed25519/Secp256k1 signing engines, and Threshold Signature Schemes (TSS).
- **Distributed Backend & Microservices** — event-driven NestJS & Rust architectures, BullMQ queues, transactional outboxes, gRPC, and PostgreSQL optimization.
- **Autonomous AI Swarms** — Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers, multi-agent closed-loop pipelines, automated code reviews, and LangGraph workflows.
